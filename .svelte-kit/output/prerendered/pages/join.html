Join the Club

Anyone can join. No sign-ups, fees, or experience necessary.

General Membership

Our club is open to anyone (seven lakes student) who wants to attend our meetings. No sign up is required and we don't keep a list of members.

Here's what you get as a general member:

Weekly Presentations

Learn about algorithms, web dev, game dev, and more from fellow students.

CP Practice

Weekly competitive programming sessions to sharpen your problem-solving skills.

Contests

Participate in local and national programming competitions throughout the year.

Community

Connect with other students who share your interest in computer science.

Computer Science Honor Society (CSHS)

Getting inducted into an Honor Society is a great way to distinguish yourself in the CS community and show your dedication to your college.

Requirements for Induction

  • Volunteer 8 hours towards our club
  • Pay a fee to support the club on Pay n Go under Computer Science Club
  • Fill out the registration Google Form
